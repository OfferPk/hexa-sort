## Full description (limit 4000)

**2609 / 4000 characters** (the emoji section markers count as 1 character each here; Play may count some as 2, which still leaves plenty of room)

```
Hexcairn is a sleek hexa sort puzzle with a satisfying 2.5D look. Drop stacks of colored hex tiles onto the board, watch matching colors slide together in chain reactions, and merge 10 of a color to clear them. It's easy to learn and hard to put down: a relaxing brain teaser for a quick break or a long session.

⬢ HOW TO PLAY
• Drag one of the 3 hex stacks in your tray onto any empty cell of the board.
• The top tiles of neighbouring stacks with the same color slide together.
• Stacks that give tiles away reveal new colors, which can slide again: chain reactions!
• A stack with 10 or more tiles of one color merges and clears for points.
• Reach the target score to complete the level. If the board fills up, the level is over.

⭐ FEATURES
• 500+ levels that ramp up smoothly: more colors, blocked cells and taller stacks as you progress.
• Every level is checked by a built-in simulator, so each one can be completed.
• A fair tray: stacks use the level's colors and there is always something useful to play when the board allows it.
• Chain combos: every extra clear in the same move multiplies your points.
• Smooth, satisfying animations: tiles flip and slide between stacks, and clears burst into pieces.
• Crisp sound effects and light haptics (both can be turned off).
• Premium themes to unlock with coins you earn by playing: Basalt, Glacier, Ember, Jade Temple and Aurora.
• Color-blind aid: optional symbols on the top tiles.
• Play offline: no Wi-Fi needed. A true no wifi game for the bus, a flight, or anywhere.
• No timer and no pressure. Your progress is saved automatically.
• Stuck? Watch an optional ad to use the Hammer, shuffle your tray, or revive once per level.

🧠 A RELAXING BRAIN TEASER
Hexa puzzles look simple, but every drop counts. Keep space free, build tall single-color stacks, and set up chains that clear several stacks in one move. If you enjoy hexa sort, stack sort, color sort, tile matching or merge puzzles, you'll feel right at home with this offline puzzle game.

💡 TIPS
• Drop stacks next to the same top color to start a slide.
• Keep empty cells around the middle of the board.
• Mixed stacks are fine: their lower colors can start the next chain.

ℹ️ GOOD TO KNOW
• Hexcairn is free to play and shows ads. Hammer, Shuffle and Revive come from optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
• There are no in-app purchases. Coins can only be earned by playing and are only used for cosmetic themes.
• No sign-up and no account.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/hexa-sort/privacy.html

Download Hexcairn and start stacking: drop, slide and merge!
```

## Claims check

Every claim matches the current game (v1.0.0):

- **500+ levels / every level can be completed:** levels are generated without limit; each level's target comes from a greedy simulation of that level, and `npm test` re-plays levels 1–500 in CI and checks each is won within its placement budget.
- **More colors, blocked cells, taller stacks as you progress:** `levelParams()` in `www/js/logic.js` (3 → 8 colors, up to 9 blocked cells, stack height up to 7).
- **Merge 10 to clear, chain multiplier:** `resolve()` in `www/js/logic.js`, unit-tested.
- **Fair tray:** `dealTray()`; tested in `test/logic.test.js`.
- **Offline:** the game runs fully offline. Ads just don't load without internet.
- **Hammer, Shuffle (once per tray) and Revive (once per level) via optional rewarded ads:** correct.
- **Coins are cosmetic only; no in-app purchases:** correct. There is no billing library in the app.
- **No account or sign-up:** correct.
